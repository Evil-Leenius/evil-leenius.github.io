3. On-device AI: what stays on your device

When you describe a world in Seed Sprout, the text is interpreted by Apple's on-device language model where your device supports it. If it does not, the app uses a fixed keyword-matching routine written into the app. In both cases the processing happens on your device. Seed Sprout has no cloud AI path: your descriptions are not sent to us, to Anthropic, or to any other AI provider.

Your description is kept only as part of a saved world (and, if you share that world to the community, in the shared record: see section 5).

Blocksmith uses an on-device AI model when you describe an object, the text is interpreted by Apple's on-device language model where your device supports it. If it does not, the app uses a fixed keyword-matching routine written into the app. In both cases the processing happens on your device. Seed Sprout has no cloud AI path: your descriptions are not sent to us, to Anthropic, or to any other AI provider.

4. Accounts, sync and sharing between the two apps

Seed Sprout

  • There is no Seed Sprout account. Where an identity is needed (community features, sync) the app uses your Apple Account through iCloud.
  • Saved-world sync uses your own private iCloud database. You can turn it off under Settings ("Sync saved worlds with iCloud"); worlds then stay on the device. Turning it off does not by itself delete copies already in iCloud: remove them in iCloud settings or delete the worlds in the app.
  • If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app still works with local storage.

Between Seed Sprout and Blocksmith on one device

When both apps are installed on the same device and signed by Wimji Ltd, they can share a protected folder (an Apple app group). Blocksmith places the models you have chosen to share in that folder, and Seed Sprout reads them so you can place your own structures in a world. Seed Sprout opens Blocksmith or is opened by it through link addresses (blocksmith:// and seedsprout://).

  • Seed Sprout only reads the shared models. Per Blocksmith's design, you must connect the apps first and can revoke the connection.
  • The shared folder holds model files, thumbnails and manifests (name, size, edition, version, hash). No account tokens, analytics or personal data are exchanged between the apps, and this exchange uses no network.
  • Another app cannot trigger an import. Each import is confirmed inside Seed Sprout.

Blocksmith

  • No account is required to use the app. You may sign in with Apple or Google to sync and back up models between your devices.
  • Signed-in devices upload a compressed copy of each model after a few seconds of inactivity. Conflicting edits are kept as separate copies. Deleted models are recoverable for 30 days.
  • Under-age users (section 7) stay local-only with no account, sync or community.
  • Account deletion inside the app removes your models within 30 days, and a data export is available. [To confirm: these are targets, not yet built.]

5. Community features and moderation

Seed Sprout community (optional, public)

Community features use a public Apple CloudKit database. Nothing is uploaded until you share a world.

When you share a world, the following becomes visible to other Seed Sprout users: the world's name (up to 40 characters), the description you typed (up to 500 characters), seed, edition, version, theme, features, start biome and city-placement settings, the date, and a display name (up to 24 characters; default "A player") that you choose. Do not put personal information in these fields.

Behind the record, CloudKit stores an identifier for your iCloud user. The app reads that identifier so you can delete your own posts and so we can tell who created a record. When you like a world, a "like" record is created whose name includes your CloudKit user identifier. When you report a world, a report record is created with a reference to the world and a reason of up to 200 characters, and the same identifier. [To confirm: who can read like and report records in the CloudKit security roles, and how long they are kept.]

  • You can delete your own shared worlds at any time.
  • Moderation: a short blocked-word list checks names and prompts; worlds with 3 or more reports are hidden by the app; you can block a player or hide a world on your device (stored locally).
  • Staff Picks are chosen by our team.
  • Posts on the public database may have been seen or copied before you delete them.

Blocksmith community (planned, not in the first release)

The first release is private: every model has an owner, and an unlisted share link would open a read-only view. A public gallery with display-name profiles, fixed-reaction likes, remixing, two-tap reporting, human review and takedown, and filters on names and tags is planned for later, once moderation and legal review are ready. Shared files hold model data and a thumbnail. They never contain our texture files. This section will be updated before the gallery launches.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Seed Sprout offers a consumable purchase (extra world builds) and an auto-renewing monthly subscription (unlimited builds), plus a free monthly build. Purchases use Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and gives the app the status of your purchase; we do not receive your card or billing details. Subscriptions renew until you cancel in your Apple Account settings. The paywall links to Apple's standard licence agreement.

Blocksmith is planned as free to build, save and export, with an optional Pro subscription and, later, optional cosmetic packs. Purchases will use the platform's own payment and parental-approval flows. There are no ads, loot boxes or countdown pressure tactics.

7. Children

Minecraft is popular with children, so both apps are designed on the assumption that some young people will use them.

  • Neither app contains advertising or behavioural tracking. Both include error reporting. [To confirm: that reports contain no personal data or user content, and that they are handled the same way for younger users.]
  • Seed Sprout has no account of its own. Its optional community features use the public CloudKit database and show a display name chosen by the person sharing. Apple's own age and parental controls for Apple Accounts apply.
  • Blocksmith is planned to show a neutral age prompt before any account can be created. Anyone under 13, or under their country's age of digital consent, stays local-only: no account, sync or community. A parent-approved account route may be added later.
  • Parents who believe a child has shared personal information through a public community post can contact us (section 11) and we will remove it.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: kept until you delete the worlds or models, or uninstall the app.
  • Seed Sprout private iCloud copies: kept in your iCloud until you delete the worlds, turn off iCloud for the app, or remove the app's iCloud data in your iCloud settings. We cannot access or delete them for you.
  • Seed Sprout community posts: kept until you delete them in the app.
  • Blocksmith cloud copies (planned): kept while your account exists. Deleted models are recoverable for 30 days; snapshots are limited to the most recent 3 (Free) or 10 (Pro); deleting your account removes all models within 30 days.

10. Your rights and choices

Under UK GDPR (and EU GDPR where it applies) you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to have it erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time.

In practice:

  • Seed Sprout: you control saved-world sync in Settings, you can delete any world you have saved or shared, and you can block players or hide worlds. Most of your data is on your device or in your private iCloud, where you manage it directly.
  • Blocksmith (planned): in-app account deletion, a data export, and sign-out at any time.
  • For anything we hold that you cannot manage yourself, contact us (section 11).

11. Security, changes and contact

Security. Your data is protected by the security of your device and Apple's services. Seed Sprout has no server of ours. For Blocksmith we plan encryption in transit and at rest and database rules that let only the owner read and write a model. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes. We will update this policy when the apps change (in particular before Blocksmith or its community features launch) and will show the date of the latest version.
