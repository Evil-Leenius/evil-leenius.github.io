1. Opening your world in Minecraft

Sprout finds a seed, and when your description needs more than a seed it also makes a world file. Save it, or open it in Minecraft straight from the app when Minecraft is on the same device.

Bedrock (.mcworld)

  1. Tap Open in Minecraft, or save the file and open it with Minecraft.
  2. Minecraft imports the world. Find it in your worlds list.

Java (.zip)

  1. On a Mac, tap Open Minecraft: the world is placed in your Minecraft saves folder for you.
  2. Otherwise, save the file and unzip it into the saves folder of your Minecraft folder.

Just the seed

Minecraft on iPhone and iPad can’t be handed a seed from outside, so type it into the new-world screen yourself, or use the world file, which already holds the seed.

Open the world in the same edition and version the app showed. A different version can place things differently.

2. Common questions

The world didn’t appear in Minecraft.
  • Bedrock: open the .mcworld file from the Files app or the share sheet and choose Minecraft.
  • Java: the unzipped folder must sit directly inside saves, not inside another folder.
  • Check there is enough free space, then try building the file again. A failed build isn’t counted against your allowance.
A structure isn’t where the app says it is.

Use the /locate command the app shows next to each item. Sprout checks structures against the real game, but some can’t be promised on every version. Those are listed as Not covered yet or Lost structures on the results screen, so you know before you build.

What does “Read with basic keyword matching” mean?

Your description was read by looking for known words rather than by Apple’s on-device AI. That happens when Apple Intelligence is off or not available on your device. With it on, more of your wording is understood. Either way, your description is interpreted on your device and is not sent to a cloud AI service for interpretation.

My Blocksmith model wasn’t found.
  • Blocksmith and Sprout must both be installed on the same device.
  • The model must be saved in Blocksmith. Name it in your description as “my Monolith Portal”, using its name or part of it.
  • If no saved model matches, Sprout builds the world without it and says so in a red notice at the top of the results.
How do the free build and purchases work?
  • Every month includes one free world build, which returns on the 1st.
  • After that you can buy a single world, or subscribe for unlimited builds.
  • Restore purchases and manage your subscription from Settings in the app; you can also cancel in your Apple Account.
  • Payments, refunds and receipts are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. We never see your card details.
My saved worlds aren’t on my other device.

Check that both devices use the same Apple Account with iCloud on, and that Sync saved worlds with iCloud is on in Settings. Syncing isn’t instant. Sprout has no account of its own.

Which devices does it run on?

Sprout is being prepared for iPhone, iPad and Mac. The current build targets iOS and iPadOS 27 or later, and macOS 27 or later. Final compatibility will be confirmed at release. Android and Windows versions are planned but not available.

Does my description leave my device?

It is interpreted on your device. Saved-world sync can copy it to your private iCloud storage, community sharing can make it public, and you can choose to include it in an emailed support report. See the privacy policy.

3. Sending us a problem report

A report helps us reproduce a problem. Nothing is sent unless you press Send in your own email app, and the app shows you exactly what it contains first.

Depending on what you choose to include, a report can contain:

  • The app version, your device model and system version, and roughly how much storage is free.
  • The error code and the screen it happened on.
  • The last few steps you took (which screens and buttons, never what you typed).
  • If you leave the switch on, the world description and settings, and what the planner decided.
  • Available crash details, if supplied by the system after the app closed unexpectedly.
  • Any reply address you choose to add. Your sending email address is also included when you send the email.

The report does not automatically attach your files, photos or screenshots. Your written message and any world description you include may contain personal information, and your email app may add your sender name, address and signature. Review the report and email before sending.

If no email app is set up, the app offers the report as a file you can attach to a message yourself.

4. Your data

  • Delete a saved world in the app to remove it. If you used iCloud sync, copies in your iCloud go when you delete the world in the app, or you can remove the app’s iCloud data in your iCloud settings.
  • We hold very little about you. For anything you can’t manage yourself, or any question about your rights, write to us and see the privacy policy.
