The creative tools we’re building for Blocksmith.

01

Every block tells a story.

Create in 3D

Build and explore your designs in a fully three-dimensional workspace. Add, remove, arrange and reshape blocks as your creation takes form.

From the tiniest details to the grandest structures, see your ideas come together from every angle.

02

A little imagination. A lot of magic.

Describe it. Build it.

Got an idea but don't know where to begin?

Imagine describing a "cosy oak cottage with a roaring fireplace, a little kitchen and a bedroom upstairs" and watching Blocksmith turn those words into a starting design.

Our planned on-device AI will help translate your ideas into Minecraft-inspired creations, with sensible architectural details and thoughtful building choices.

Less time figuring out where to start. More time making it yours.

03

The details make the difference.

Shape, refine and perfect

Sometimes, moving a wall by one block changes everything.

We're designing intuitive editing tools to help you select, move, rotate, duplicate and refine your creations with precision.

Build carefully, experiment wildly, undo your mistakes and keep going until everything feels just right.

04

Make it unmistakably yours.

Materials, colours and creativity

A house is a house. Until you make it your house.

Experiment with different block materials, textures, architectural styles and decorative details. Turn a plain structure into a rustic hideaway, a grand palace or a futuristic masterpiece.

The same idea can become a thousand different creations.

05

Think bigger. Build smarter.

From tiny details to epic builds

A little garden shed? Absolutely.

An enormous fortress with towers, courtyards and hidden passageways? Now we're talking.

Blocksmith is being designed to support everything from simple decorative models to more ambitious structures, with tools to make complex creations easier to manage.

Because your ideas shouldn't have to fit inside a tiny box.

06

Your creations. Ready for adventure.

Design for Minecraft

A great build deserves more than a place in your imagination.

Our goal is to make it straightforward to take the models and structures you design in Blocksmith into compatible Minecraft workflows, without needing to become an expert in technical file formats.
